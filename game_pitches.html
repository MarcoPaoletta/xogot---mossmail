01 / 3D GAME CONCEPT

MOSSMAIL

A cozy forest delivery adventure

PLAY LOOP

Glide between giant trees, deliver lost letters, and restore a forgotten woodland postal route.

THE HOOK

Movement-first exploration with tiny stories, no combat, and a forest that lights up as you reconnect it.

Scope, assets & build risks

FIRST PLAYABLE

One grove · three delivery stops · one glider upgrade · 8–10 minutes.

PROPOSED ASSET ROUTE

Trees, rocks and ground plants: Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack. Courier: original simple character. No rigged animal assumed.
